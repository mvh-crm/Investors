📱 Setting Up Twilio (Step-by-Step)

Follow these steps to enable SMS reminders.

  1. Go to twilio.com/try-twilio and create a free account.
  2. Verify your personal phone number during signup — this becomes your recipient number.
  3. From the Twilio Console dashboard, copy your Account SID and Auth Token and paste them above.
  4. Click Get a Twilio phone number in the Console to receive a free number. Paste it in the "Twilio Phone Number" field above.
  5. With a free trial account, you can only SMS verified numbers. Your personal number is already verified from step 2.
  6. That's it — click "Test SMS" below to verify it works.

🔑 Getting a Claude API Key

Required for business card scanning. Takes 2 minutes.

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in.
  2. Click API Keys in the left sidebar.
  3. Click Create Key, give it a name like "Investor CRM".
  4. Copy the key (starts with sk-ant-) and paste it in the Claude API Key field above.
  5. Add a small credit ($5–$10) under Billing — business card scans cost fractions of a cent each.
